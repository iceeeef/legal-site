Icef Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026.

Your records

Icef stores your ice day descriptions, hole records, catches, condition observations, rotation plans, photos, video, audio, voice notes, recap files, preferences, and purchase delivery records in this app on your device. Icef does not require an account.

Permissions and media

With your permission, the camera captures photos and video, the microphone records audio and voice notes, and your photo library provides selected media or receives an exported recap. You can decline permission and continue using other features. Speech recognition may send recorded audio to Apple when on-device recognition is unavailable. You may type notes instead.

Third-party processing

Apple processes purchases and system speech recognition. Icef does not sell your personal data or use third-party advertising services. Sharing a recap transfers it only to the destination you choose. Please agree to a destination's own terms before sharing. The website host may process ordinary connection information when you view this policy or the terms.

Notifications

Icef requests the system notification permission when you first reach the ice day home screen. You can change this permission in device settings.

Retention and deletion

Delete individual ice days or use Clear All Data to remove records and remaining Reel Passes from this device. Minimal transaction identifiers are retained to prevent duplicate delivery of purchases. Deleting the app removes its local files. No account recovery or cross-device synchronization is provided.

Your choices

You control camera, photo, microphone, speech and notification permissions through device settings. Export or share your recap using the app. Do not submit personal information belonging to another person without their permission.
